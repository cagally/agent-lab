# Go-To-Market (GTM)

This folder contains go-to-market strategy, content marketing materials, growth hacking tactics, and campaign plans.

## Phase 2 Focus (Days 4 - 8 Weeks)

Phase 2 centers on driving traffic and building a following through strategic marketing efforts. The approach includes social media presence across relevant platforms, community engagement on Reddit and other forums, influencer partnerships and outreach, content marketing with valuable insights, and direct outreach to companies and potential partners.

## Growth Channels

**Organic:** SEO-optimized content, community engagement, word-of-mouth  
**Partnerships:** Influencer collaborations, company partnerships  
**Paid (if needed):** Targeted ads to accelerate growth  

## Success Metrics

Traffic growth, user engagement, skill discovery usage, community building, and brand awareness are the key indicators of success during this phase.
